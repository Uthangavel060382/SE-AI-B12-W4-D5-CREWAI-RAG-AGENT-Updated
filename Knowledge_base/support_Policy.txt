
CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY
Company: ABC Electronics
Version: Demo 1.0

RETURN POLICY
Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.
Products must be in their original condition with all accessories.
Customers must provide an order number or proof of purchase.

REFUND POLICY
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
Refunds generally take 5 to 7 business days after approval.
Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the company shipped
the incorrect product or a defective product.

SHIPPING POLICY
Standard shipping takes approximately 5 to 7 business days.
Express shipping takes approximately 2 to 3 business days.

WARRANTY POLICY
ABC Electronics provides a one-year limited warranty
covering manufacturing defects under normal use.
Accidental damage is not covered by the standard warranty.

CONTACT SUPPORT
Customers can request assistance through the support portal.
An order number should be provided for order-related issues.
